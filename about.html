About Rynix Salon

A premium bridal beauty studio dedicated to making every bride feel radiant and confident.

At Rynix Salon we believe your wedding day look should feel as special as the moment itself. We specialise in bridal makeup, hair transformations and complete beauty experiences using premium products and meticulous techniques.

From the first consultation to the final touch-up, our focus is on understanding your vision, your skin and hair needs, and delivering a look that photographs beautifully and lasts through every celebration.
